RULE 001✳

FAITH & PURPOSE

Keep God above all.

“Never allow the pursuit of a woman, the weight of heartbreak, the hunger for success, or the opinions of men to take the place of God in your life. A man grounded in God may bend beneath the pressure of life, but he will never have to walk through the fire alone.”

LETTERS TO MY BROTHERS · 001

To the brother who keeps saying, “I'm fine.”

I hope you know that you do not have to carry every burden alone. You can be grateful for your life and still admit that it is heavy. You can be strong and still need a place to rest.

Do not measure your worth by who stayed, who left, or how quickly you recovered. You are still becoming. Take responsibility for your healing, ask for help when you need it, and give yourself permission to begin again.

Keep building the man you want to become, even when nobody is clapping. And when your brother is the one struggling, be the person who reminds him that he does not have to face the fire alone.

With respect, one brother to another.

THE FURNALATIONS · OUR OATH

We will not let the fire
make us heartless.

01 We will choose honesty over pretending.

02 We will protect our peace without losing our compassion.

03 We will hold each other accountable without humiliation.

04 We will honour loyalty, boundaries, and truth.

05 We will grow through pain, not build an identity around it.

06 We will ask for help and offer it without shame.
